# FrontDesk positioning — 8 September 2026

This direction supersedes the earlier screenshot-led landing page.

1. Lead with automatic collection and reconciliation: the amount owed is sent to the tenant, the tenant pays via a connected provider, the provider's payment reference identifies the tenant, and the confirmed payment updates the ledger automatically. The owner does not review and post each integrated payment.
2. Sell an implementation tailored to the client's business and market. User-named examples are Razorpay and Cashfree. Do not imply globally available, already-connected production integrations from a static marketing demo.
3. Owner and tenant messaging assistants are the second main feature. Avoid naming the messaging platform on the website. Owner-directed actions are reviewed before execution; this does not apply to automatic recording of confirmed integrated payments.
4. Screenshot reading, forwarded bank alerts and cash recording are alternatives outside the integrated flow and may require review.
5. Keep copy concise, use the original sans-serif/italic hero treatment, and do not restore the units or confirmation claims in the hero. Avoid invented customer counts and performance metrics.

Provider terminology checked against [Razorpay Smart Collect documentation](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/smart-collect/): customer identifiers, automatic reconciliation, received-payment notifications and merchant prerequisites. Cashfree availability was not independently verified in this session (official pages returned 404/403); it is mentioned as an implementation option per the user's product direction, without specific rail or settlement promises.
